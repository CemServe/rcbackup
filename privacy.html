Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Rclone Backup is used only by its owner, to back up their own files to their own Google Drive.

Data accessed

The app accesses only the Google Drive of the account that signs in to it. It reads and writes backup files in that Drive.

Data use and sharing

No data is collected, sold or shared. Data only moves between the owner's own systems and the owner's own Google Drive. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and removal

Sign-in credentials are kept only on the owner's own systems. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
